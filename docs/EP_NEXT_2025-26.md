## Caveats

- 2025-26 is our sealed hold-out season and has been looked at before (docs/XP_EVALUATION_sealed.md), though the model was not tuned on it.

- FPL's copy is 0.4-10 hours before the deadline; ours uses deadline information but no team news (no availability flags exist for past seasons in our features), so FPL has an information advantage on injuries and suspensions.

- Players outside our universe are predicted 0 by us; FPL gives some of them a positive ep_next.
